How it works

1 The field is hexagons. You can only take one that touches a hexagon you hold, so you always start at the rim and work inward.
2 Tap one to play. Five questions, twelve seconds each, three right and it is yours. Some rounds are true or false, some are a ladder you can bank or lose.
3 Every win pays climb points. Enough of them and you go up a level, and the questions get harder. There are fifty levels and twelve sections.
4 Hold ten subjects at most. Anyone who beats you takes one, and a knockback costs you climb points, so the top is a long haul, not an evening.
