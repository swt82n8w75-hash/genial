**Description:**
Geniality is a quiz you climb rather than finish.

Start at the rim with the broad subjects and work inward. Every subject is a hexagon on a lattice, and you can only reach the ones that touch where you stand. Win three of five rounds to claim a hexagon and hold it. Hold it and it is yours to play as often as you like.

Five questions a round, twelve seconds each, and the clock pays: the first third of the time is worth the most. Some rounds are true or false. Some are ladders that get harder as they climb. Some ask you to find three in a row.

Fifty levels in twelve sections, from the Ground to the Crown. Losing a hexagon costs climb points, and enough of them knocks you back a section. Nobody reaches the top in a fortnight, and that is the point.

Play the computer, play a friend by challenge link, or race somebody live and take the point for the fastest right answer.

Write your own questions too. Three good ones open a new subject, and the quizmaster grades them and writes the rest.

No adverts. No subscription. Buy the levels once and they are yours.
