# Geniality privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Geniality is a quiz game. Top Line Support Ltd publishes it.

**What we collect.** The name you choose to play under, the subjects you hold, the questions you write, your score and your rating. Nothing else.

**What we do not collect.** No email address, no phone number, no location, no contacts, no photographs, no advertising identifier. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

**Where it is kept.** On the game server that runs Geniality, and on your own device so the game opens without a connection.

**Deleting it.** Ask us and we will delete your player record, your questions and your scores. Write to james@toplinesupport.co.uk.

**Children.** Geniality is a general quiz game and is not directed at children under 13.

**Contact.** james@toplinesupport.co.uk, Top Line Support Ltd, 26 Totterdown Street, London SW17 8TA.
